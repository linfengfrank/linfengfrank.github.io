Research Projects

Selected projects from my academic and industry experience, as documented in my 2026 CV.

Funded Research Projects

  • Laser-aided Stereo Vision Study (2014–2015). With Ben M. Chen (PI) and K. M. Peng, Temasek Laboratories, NUS. Funding: S$50,000.
  • Reconfigurable multi-attitude autonomous flight control system design (2014–2015). With Ben M. Chen (PI) and K. M. Peng, Temasek Laboratories, NUS. Funding: S$50,000.
  • Vision System for Micro UAVs (2013–2014). With Ben M. Chen, K. M. Peng, and F. Liao, Temasek Laboratories, NUS. Funding: S$50,000.

Other Research Involvement

  • Special Project for DARPA UAVForge Competition — DSO National Laboratories, January–May 2012.
  • Development of Multi-UAV Testbeds and Vision-Based Navigation and Motion Coordination — Temasek Laboratories, NUS, 2009–2013.
  • Guidance and Control of Multiple Air-Vehicle System — Defence Science and Technology Agency, 2009–2013.

Industry: Sea-and-Air Drone Development

As Chief Technology Officer at Shenzhen MicroHiggs Technologies (2019–2022), I led development work on a fishfinder drone for sea and air operations, working with cross-functional teams on UAV design, prototyping, testing, and sonar integration.
