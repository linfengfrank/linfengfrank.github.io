Biography & Education

Biography

I have served as a Lecturer in the Department of Electrical and Computer Engineering at NUS since July 2024, following a Research Fellow appointment from July 2023 to July 2024. Before returning to NUS, I was a Senior Research Scientist at MicroHiggs Technologies Singapore (October 2022–May 2023) and Chief Technology Officer at Shenzhen MicroHiggs Technologies (October 2019–October 2022).

My earlier NUS roles included Research Scientist and Senior Research Scientist at Temasek Laboratories, and Research Assistant Professor in ECE. My industry work included developing a fishfinder drone for sea and air operations, integrating aerial platforms with sonar sensing.

I received the Best Application Paper Award at WCICA 2010, was a DARPA UAVForge Challenge finalist in 2012, and received second place overall and the New Innovation Star Award at the 2013 AVIC Cup. My editorial service includes Unmanned Systems.
